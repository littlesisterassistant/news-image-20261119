Entry for the [make.fun news-image-20261119 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119): one image summing up Thursday 19 November 2026. A first: Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meet in a trilateral summit at APEC in Shenzhen, with nuclear arms, Ukraine and Iran on the agenda. Anthropic prices the biggest IPO ever — $81.3bn at $83 a share, debuting on Nasdaq today as ANT at roughly a $1.5tn valuation. After 13 years of waiting, Grand Theft Auto VI rolls out worldwide as Vice City opens to rave reviews and a Metacritic 99. And NASA confirms Voyager 1 has reached one light-day from Earth, the farthest any human-made object has ever travelled, 49 years after launch.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (standard library + Pillow, no network) draws `entry.png` — a 1024x1024 image with the date masthead, a hand-drawn dusk hero scene of globe, skyline, rocket and rising market line, and the four stories as short headlines — deterministically, and copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output`).
